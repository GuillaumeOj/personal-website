Welcome to this blog. I had been telling myself for years that "I should keep a personal site," and
this week I finally took the time to do it.

## Why a blog

I have been a Python backend developer for several years, and more recently I have started
training in project management. Keeping a blog lets me:

- track what I am learning, especially around the dev → project manager transition;
- share notes on specific backend topics (FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, observability);
- think out loud — writing to clarify your ideas is an old trick, but it works.

## What you will find here

No strict schedule. A few short posts per month if all goes well, in French and English. The
source code of this site lives on GitHub — feel free to take a look if you are curious about the
stack (Astro, Tailwind, deployed on Vercel).

See you around.
