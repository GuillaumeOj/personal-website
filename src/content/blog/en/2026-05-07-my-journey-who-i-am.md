Welcome to this blog. This first post is my way of introducing myself — not with a résumé, but with the real lines of my story.

## Where I come from

I've always been drawn to two things that seem at odds: building concrete stuff, and understanding how systems work.

Before tech, I was a structural draftsman in reinforced concrete engineering offices. You draw so that things can actually be built and executed in the real world, on the construction site. What I found most interesting about the job was that the structural engineering office is just one piece of the puzzle — you have to work across all the different trades, each with their own objectives and constraints. Learning to navigate those interfaces is probably what I took away most from that time.

The move into software development was a deliberate choice: to discover new ways of building things. Not concrete and steel, but systems, interfaces, logic. The core impulse is the same.

## The entrepreneurial shift

At some point, writing code for others wasn't enough anymore. I wanted to build something of my own.

Two reasons gave birth to Fusily. The first is a simple observation: making good food choices in the vastness of the internet is hard. Turning those choices into a coherent shopping list, and then actually getting into the kitchen — there's a real barrier between the knowledge available online and what ends up on the stove. The idea behind Fusily is to remove that barrier: to connect what you find on the internet to what you cook at home.

The second reason is more personal: the desire to take a project from A to Z. To deal with more than just writing code — product, strategy, communication, users. To own a project at the macro level, and see what that's actually like.

## Why this blog

I'm writing here to share what I learn along the way. Entrepreneurship, tech, product, hard decisions, instructive mistakes — everything that makes up the real texture of a journey, far from polished success stories.

No ready-made lessons. Just honest reflections.

Welcome behind the scenes.
